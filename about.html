Our Heritage & Philosophy

Precision Engineering Meets Motorsport Passion

New England Drive was founded to bridge the gap between real-world automotive motorsport performance and commercial-grade simulator environments.

We work closely with track drivers, automotive enthusiasts, and dedicated sim racers to source, configure, and integrate high-end hardware systems that replicate true vehicle dynamics.

From individual component recommendations to complete bespoke rig design and integration, our approach prioritizes craftsmanship, engineering precision, and ongoing client support.
